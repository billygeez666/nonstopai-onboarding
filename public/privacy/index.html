Cookies and storage

Service pages, sample websites and this page. They don’t set cookies or load advertising trackers. The enquiry form stores campaign tags in your browser’s session storage for the length of your visit so we can attribute your enquiry; it is cleared when you close the tab.

Main site (homepage and sign-up pages). We use the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel to measure our advertising. It is off by default: nothing is loaded from Meta unless you click “Accept” on the cookie banner. If you accept, Meta may set cookies (such as _fbp and _fbc) and receive information about the pages you view and the enquiries you send; Meta handles that data under its own privacy policy. If you click “Reject”, nothing is loaded. We store your choice in your browser’s local storage for up to 180 days and then ask again. You can change your mind at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer of the homepage.
