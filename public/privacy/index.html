Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026. This policy explains how NONSTOP AI handles personal data on this website, through our enquiry and sign-up forms, when you call our phone lines, and when we contact businesses about our services.

Who we are

Sando Aleksanders, trading as NONSTOP AI, is the controller of your personal data. Contact: aibookingsystems@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: name, business name, email, optional phone number and message, and the service you’re interested in.
  • Source information sent with the form: the page you used and, if present in the link, campaign tags (UTM values, click ID) and the referring site.
  • Sign-up and onboarding: your business details and the information you give us so we can set up your service. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we do not see or store your card number.
  • Phone calls: if you call our demo or business phone lines, you are speaking to an AI receptionist. We handle your phone number and what you say so the call can be answered and your details passed to us. Some calls may be recorded and transcribed by our voice-AI providers, and we may store the transcript, a summary and a link to the recording in our customer database.
  • Business contact details: for businesses we may contact about our services, we hold the business name, address, phone number, website and a business email address, collected from public sources such as business websites and online listings.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and set up and run the service you ask for (steps at your request before a contract, and performing a contract).
  • To answer your call, take your details and check how well the service is working (legitimate interests).
  • To contact businesses about our services, with a way to opt out in every email (legitimate interests).
  • To understand which pages and campaigns lead to enquiries, and to keep our systems secure and reliable (legitimate interests).
  • To measure our advertising with the Meta Pixel, only if you accept advertising cookies (consent).

We don’t sell your data. The enquiry form does not take payment.

Who processes it

We use these providers to run the service. They process data on our instructions, apart from Meta, which acts under its own policy if you accept advertising cookies.

  • Netlify — website hosting and form submissions.
  • Google (Gmail) — email notifications and replies.
  • n8n — automation that routes enquiry notifications to us.
  • Supabase — database for customer, lead and call records. Our database is hosted in Ireland (EU).
  • Stripe — payment processing for customers who sign up.
  • Bland AI and Vapi — voice-AI providers that run our AI receptionist and demo phone lines, including any call recording and transcription.
  • Twilio — telephone numbers and call routing.
  • Meta — only if you accept advertising cookies.

Transfers outside the UK

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. What we have confirmed from their published terms: Netlify’s data processing terms rely on its certification to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the UK Extension, with the UK International Data Transfer Agreement as a fallback; Stripe’s data transfer terms cover UK transfers under the Data Privacy Framework and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum; Bland AI’s data processing terms use standard contractual clauses together with the UK International Data Transfer Agreement; Vapi states that it processes data in the United States. For the other providers listed above, their locations and safeguards are not described here — contact us for details.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to a customer relationship: our policy is to delete them 12 months after our last contact with you. Spam or junk submissions: our policy is to delete them within 30 days. We currently carry out these deletions manually, not automatically.
  • Accounting and invoicing records for customers: kept for six years where the law requires it.
  • Call recordings and transcripts: we do not yet have an automatic deletion schedule for these, so we keep them until we delete them. If you ask us to delete yours, we will delete our copy and ask the relevant voice-AI provider to delete theirs. Our voice-AI providers may also hold call data under their own terms. In particular, Vapi’s published privacy policy says it may retain call recordings, transcripts and logs and use them to improve its AI models, with retention depending on how a customer configures their account. We have not yet confirmed the settings on our own Vapi account, so we cannot say whether any of your call data is used in that way; we will update this policy when we have.
  • Business contact details: we keep them while we consider the business a relevant contact. If you opt out, we keep a minimal record (your email address or phone number) so we do not contact you again.
  • If you ask us to delete your data, we do so without undue delay and normally within one month, unless the law requires or allows us to keep it.
  • We keep data for longer only where needed for a legal claim, complaint or legal obligation, and only until that is resolved.

Cookies and storage

Service pages, sample websites and this page. They don’t set cookies or load advertising trackers. The enquiry form stores campaign tags in your browser’s session storage for the length of your visit so we can attribute your enquiry; it is cleared when you close the tab.

Main site (homepage and sign-up pages). We use the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel to measure our advertising. It is off by default: nothing is loaded from Meta unless you click “Accept” on the cookie banner. If you accept, Meta may set cookies (such as _fbp and _fbc) and receive information about the pages you view and the enquiries you send; Meta handles that data under its own privacy policy. If you click “Reject”, nothing is loaded. We store your choice in your browser’s local storage for up to 180 days and then ask again. You can change your mind at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer of the homepage.

Sample websites

The example websites shown on our homepage are demonstrations of what we build. They are not live businesses, and any names, prices and details in them are illustrative.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data (including a call recording or transcript), ask us to restrict or move it, or object to how we use it, by contacting us above. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
