Privacy policy

Last updated: [date on approval]. This policy covers the NONSTOP AI service enquiry forms on the Product Video Ads and Business Automation pages.

Who we are

Sando Aleksanders, trading as NONSTOP AI, is the controller of your personal data. Contact: aibookingsystems@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Details you enter: name, business name, email, optional phone number and message, and the service you’re interested in.
  • Basic source information sent with the form: the page you used and, if present in the link, campaign tags (UTM values, click ID) and the referring site.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you wish, prepare a quote (legitimate interests / steps at your request before a contract).
  • To understand which pages and campaigns lead to enquiries (legitimate interests).

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t take payment through these forms.

Who processes it

Form submissions are received through Netlify, our website host, and then handled by us. Enquiry notifications are delivered to us by email (Gmail).

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to a customer relationship: deleted 12 months after our last contact with you.
  • Spam or junk submissions: deleted within 30 days.
  • If you ask us to delete your data, we do so without undue delay and normally within one month, unless the law requires or allows us to keep it.
  • Accounting and invoicing records for customers are kept for six years where the law requires it.
  • We keep data for longer only where needed for a legal claim, complaint or legal obligation, and only until that is resolved.

Cookies and storage

These two pages don’t set cookies or load advertising trackers. The form stores campaign tags in your browser’s session storage for the length of your visit so we can attribute your enquiry; it is cleared when you close the tab. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by contacting us above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
