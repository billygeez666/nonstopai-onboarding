Current (until incorporation): the business is operated personally. Controller wording used publicly:
"Sando Aleksanders, trading as NONSTOP AI" (subject to the owner confirming this matches his legal name).
Do NOT describe NONSTOP AI as an incorporated company or show a company number until one is issued.

## On this website (only one public place)
- `public/privacy/index.html`: the controller sentence under "Who we are" (marked `LEGAL-IDENTITY`). Also review "Last updated".
- Postal address must NOT appear on the public site (outreach emails only).

## Outside the website (not in this deploy; update at incorporation)
- Outreach email footers: `email-templates/ai-receptionist-v1.html` and `.txt` (sender identity + postal address, PECR). `public/ai-receptionist/email-preview/index.html` mirrors it with the address masked.
- ICO data protection fee: registration is per controller. A company is a new controller, so it needs its own registration (and its own tier/fee assessment).
- Accounts/contracts to move or re-paper to the company: Stripe, Netlify, Supabase, n8n, Google Workspace/Gmail, Meta, Bland, Vapi, Twilio (and phone numbers), domain, bank. Check each provider's data processing terms are accepted by the company.
- Customer-facing: tell existing customers when the contracting party changes; update the privacy policy controller line and date the same day.
- Companies House number and registered office only after they exist; do not publish placeholders.
